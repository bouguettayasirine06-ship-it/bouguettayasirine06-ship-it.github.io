About

Sirine Bouguettaya is a Ph.D. candidate in Information and Communication Technology at the Department of DIMES, University of Calabria, Italy. She holds an M.Sc. in Computer Science Systems from the University of 8 May 1945 Guelma, Algeria.

Her main research interests include Generative AI in Education, Multi-Agent Systems (MAS), Multi-Agent Reinforcement Learning (MARL), and Agentic AI. Her research focuses on developing intelligent, adaptive, and collaborative educational systems to support personalized learning and enhance teaching and learning processes.

She is particularly interested in the integration of intelligent agents and generative AI to build proactive and explainable educational environments.
