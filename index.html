About

I am a PhD candidate in Information and Communication Technologies at the University of Calabria, where I design multi-agent and agentic AI systems that support each learner continuously, in class and beyond it.

I am part of the XL (40th) cycle of the PhD programme at DIMES, supervised by Prof. Giancarlo Fortino and co-supervised by Prof. Francesco Pupo. My doctoral project, Engineering Proactive and Continuous Educational Intelligence through Multi-Agent and Agentic AI, studies how teams of cooperating AI agents can plan, deliver and evaluate personalized learning over time.

Before Calabria, I completed my Bachelor's and Master's in Computer Science at the University of 8 May 1945 Guelma, Algeria, graduating first in my Master's cohort. My earlier work focused on adaptive and secure educational social networks.

Research

RESEARCH INITIATIVE · 2025–PRESENT

Generative AI integration in Italian high schools

An ongoing study of how generative AI enters Italian secondary education: AI-assisted teaching practices, how students interact with generative tools, and the educational impact of adoption, with the aim of informing evidence-based guidelines for responsible use in schools.
